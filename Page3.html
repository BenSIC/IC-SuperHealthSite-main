On-campus support

There are many resources available for physical health on campus, including:

  • Chesly Health and Wellness Center, which provides free medical care to students
  • Campus Athletics, which provides fitness classes and intramural sports
  • Campus Dining, which provides healthy food options on campus
  • Campus Safety, which provides safety tips and resources for students
