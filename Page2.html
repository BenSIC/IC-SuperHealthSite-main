On-campus support

There are many resources available for mental health on campus, including:

  • Free counseling at Chesly Health and Wellness Center
  • NAMI on campus, which supports students with mental health issues
  • BetterMynd, an online mental health platform
  • Academic coaching services for students seeking academic and personal support
