There are many resources available for mental health on campus.

Some of these resources include:

  • Free counseling at Chesly Health And Wellness Center
  • NAMI on campus, who support students with mental health issues
  • BetterMynd, online mental health platform
  • Acedemic coaching services, available for students seeking academic and personal support
